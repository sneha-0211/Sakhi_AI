# SAKHI AI: TWO-MINUTE COMPETITION DEMONSTRATION SCRIPT
**Tagline:** "Your Voice. Your Safety. Your Space."  
**Target Duration:** 120 Seconds (2 Minutes)  
**Persona:** Fictional 14-year-old student ("Ananya") facing repeated classroom teasing and online rumors.

---

## Video Timeline & Stage Directions

### 0:00 – 0:15 | Scene 1: Introduction & Brand Purpose
- **Visual:** Open on the SAKHI AI Welcome screen (`/welcome`) displaying the custom Shield + Heart SVG branding, soothing lavender/teal theme, and clear limits of confidentiality.
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "Every day, millions of schoolgirls face bullying, academic pressure, cyber harassment, or personal boundary violations—yet hesitate to tell an adult for fear of retaliation, blame, or not being believed.  
  > Welcome to **SAKHI AI**: an interactive, AI-powered school well-being and safety companion designed specifically for students aged 10 to 18 to privately express their concerns and receive safe, age-sensitive guidance without having to initially confront an adult."

---

### 0:15 – 0:35 | Scene 2: Fictional Concern Entry & Real-Time Chat
- **Visual:** Transition to the Home Dashboard (`/`). Show category cards (Bullying, Online Safety, Boundaries, Exam Stress). Click **"Talk to SAKHI"** (`/chat`).
- **Interaction:**
  - Type the fictional prompt:
    `"Some girls in my class make fun of me every day during lunch. They laugh at my clothes and tell others not to sit with me."`
  - Click **Send**. Show the typing indicator.
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "Students never enter their real name or student ID. Here, a fictional student describes repeated lunchroom teasing in her own words. SAKHI's deterministic reasoning engine immediately parses the message locally without depending on costly or unpredictable external APIs."

---

### 0:35 – 0:55 | Scene 3: Context Gathering & Empathetic Validation
- **Visual:** The AI bubble renders with:
  1. Empathetic validation: *"I'm sorry you are experiencing this. Being teased or excluded hurts deeply, and you don't deserve that treatment."*
  2. Gentle clarifying inquiry: *"Has this been going on for days, weeks, or longer?"*
  3. Interactive suggestion chips and a discrete **"Skip this question"** button.
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "Notice how SAKHI responds: zero victim-blaming, zero forced confrontation. It offers gentle response chips while always letting the student skip questions if she feels uncomfortable."

---

### 0:55 – 1:15 | Scene 4: Transparent Urgency & Safety Assessment
- **Visual:** Click **"View Safety Assessment"** (`/assessment`). Display the structured evaluation card:
  - Urgency Tier: **MODERATE (Timely Support Recommended)** in a gentle amber badge (not alarming or medicalized).
  - Neutral Summary: Clear, non-judgmental description.
  - Transparent Indicators: *"High frequency: repeated daily occurrence"*, *"Direct verbal teasing"*, *"Organized peer exclusion"*.
  - Status: *"Adult Support Recommended"*.
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "SAKHI evaluates context across four transparent tiers: Low, Moderate, High, and Urgent. It distinguishes everyday friendship squabbles from repeated bullying and critical emergencies. The engine explicitly notes the indicators that informed the assessment, giving students clarity rather than clinical labels."

---

### 1:15 – 1:35 | Scene 5: Personalized Guidance & The Conversation Starter Generator
- **Visual:** Click **"View Personalized Action Plan & Scripts"** (`/guidance`). Show the 5-part plan:
  1. What We Understood
  2. What You Can Do Next (document incidents, keep safe buffers)
  3. Interactive **"What to say when asking for help"** script generator. Switch between **Teacher**, **School Counselor**, and **Parent**. Click **"Copy Script"**.
  4. What to do if the situation gets worse.
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "The most intimidating part for a student is figuring out what to say. SAKHI provides an interactive conversation starter generator. With one tap, she gets a safe, private script: *'I have something important to tell you. Something has been happening at school that makes me uncomfortable. Could you listen privately and help me decide what to do?'* No messages are ever sent automatically—the student stays in complete control."

---

### 1:35 – 1:50 | Scene 6: Emergency Helplines & Crisis Directory
- **Visual:** Click **"Need Urgent Help?"** in the top navigation bar (`/emergency`). Show the verified Indian directory:
  - **112**: National Emergency Response Support System (Police, Ambulance)
  - **1098**: CHILDLINE / Child Protection
  - **1091**: Women & Girls Helpline
  - **1930**: National Cyber Crime Portal
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "If a student signals immediate danger or harassment, SAKHI prioritizes safety over chatbot banter. Our verified emergency directory provides direct access to 112 and Childline 1098, with explicit clarity that SAKHI does not replace live rescue dispatch."

---

### 1:50 – 2:00 | Scene 7: Privacy-First Architecture & Conclusion
- **Visual:** Show the Privacy Center (`/privacy`), clicking **"Erase Session Data Now"**, then cut to the full stack architecture running with 18 passing Pytest tests and Capacitor Android packaging.
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "Privacy is built into the architecture: zero databases, session-only memory, automatic PII redaction, and 100% offline rule-based validation. SAKHI AI is available on mobile web and packaged for Android.  
  > SAKHI AI: Your Voice. Your Safety. Your Space."

## Quick Judge Scenario Walkthrough Matrix

Judges can explore each of the 6 standardized scenarios under **"Demo Cases"** (`/scenarios`):

| Case # | Scenario Name | Input Message Sample | Engine Urgency | Key Safety Action |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Case 1** | Everyday Exam Stress | *"I have exams next week and feel anxious and can't sleep."* | **LOW** | 25-minute Pomodoro study pacing, 4-7-8 breathing, reassuring self-worth. |
| **Case 2** | Classroom Teasing | *"Some girls in my class make fun of me every day during lunch."* | **MODERATE** | Validate hurt, note pattern, draft teacher conversation script, do not retaliate. |
| **Case 3** | Cyberbullying | *"Someone made an anonymous Instagram account posting mean memes."* | **MODERATE / HIGH** | Take screenshots, do not reply/escalate, report to platform, inform IT counselor. |
| **Case 4** | Uncomfortable Boundary | *"An older boy at tuition keeps following me and saying weird things about my body."* | **HIGH** | Break secrecy trap, 'No, Go, Tell' safety rule, transit safety plan, immediate adult intervention. |
| **Case 5** | Threatening Message | *"Someone said if I don't give them money by tomorrow they will leak fake pictures."* | **HIGH** | Anti-extortion warning (never pay/comply), preserve screenshots, approach cyber helpline 1930. |
| **Case 6** | Immediate Danger | *"Someone is trying to break into the school washroom while I'm inside. I am trapped."* | **URGENT** | Immediate safety alert, shout for help, lock door, dial 112 / 1098 immediately. |
